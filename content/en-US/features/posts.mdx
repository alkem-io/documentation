# Posts

Posts help communities **share knowledge**, **start discussions** and **structure their work**, directly **within a Space or Subspace** instead of in separate documents or tools. Use them to share news, ask questions or gather input from members.

## Basic Post

A basic post has a title and an optional description. In the description you can format text and add images, embedded media and references, such as links to webpages or documents. It's ideal for discussions, announcements and quick feedback.

Want to show a video or other content from another website inside your Post? Read how on the [iFrame page](/features/iframe).

![Basic Post](/basic-post.png)

## Additional Content

When you create a Post, you can add one extra piece of content to it under **Add to Post**: a whiteboard, memo, document, call to action, media gallery, poll or form. After the post is created you can still remove it, but you can't swap it for a different kind. A form is the exception: it stays with the post (see [Form](#form)).

### Whiteboard

A Whiteboard lets members **sketch, add notes and map out ideas together**, at the same time. You can start from a template and attach references, like webpages or documents. It's useful for brainstorming, planning and stakeholder mapping.

> In a free Space, one person can work on a whiteboard at a time. Want to work on it together at the same time? [Contact the team](/support).

![A Post with a whiteboard](/post-with-whiteboard.png)

Read more about using whiteboards on the [Whiteboard Page](/how-to/whiteboards)

### Memo

A Memo is a text that several people can write in at the same time, with formatting such as headings, lists and links. Changes are saved as you type. It's useful for meeting notes, project documentation and other writing you do together.

> In a free Space, one person can write in a memo at a time. Want to write in it together at the same time? [Contact the team](/support).

![Create a Post with a memo](/post-with-memo.png)

#### Signing a memo

In Spaces where it's turned on, you can sign a memo with your digital identity through **Cleverbase**. This only works if you have a Cleverbase account that's linked to your Alkemio account; see [Connect or disconnect a sign-in method](/how-to/connected-accounts).

Open the memo and choose **Sign memo**. Alkemio first shows you the exact PDF copy of the memo that will be signed. Check it, click **Continue to Cleverbase**, confirm your identity there, and you return to Alkemio with the result.

The signed PDF is saved with the memo under **Signed copies**, where anyone with access can open, download or **verify** it. The memo itself stays editable: signing only creates a separate PDF copy, so if you sign again after changes, you get a new copy and earlier ones are kept.

### Document

A Document adds a text document, spreadsheet or presentation to your post, which members can open and **edit together at the same time** without leaving Alkemio. You can create a new one or upload an existing Word, Excel or PowerPoint file to edit it together. A PDF you upload here can be viewed, but not edited. Documents are an extra feature that is turned on per Space. Read more on the [Documents page](/features/documents).

### Call to Action

A Call to Action (CTA) is a clickable button that guides users to perform a specific action, such as visiting a webpage, submitting a form, or starting a process.

Key Features:
- Customizable button text and link destination
- Supports external URLs or internal navigation
- Ideal for prompting sign-ups, downloads, or navigation

![Create a Post with a CTA](/post-with-cta.png)

### Poll

A Poll lets you gather structured input from your community by asking members to vote on the available options. Polls are ideal for making decisions together, gauging sentiment, or prioritising ideas. Vote counts and percentages update live as members vote.

Members can change their vote at any time as long as the poll is open. When voting is done, you can close the poll with **Close poll**, and reopen it later if needed.

When creating a poll, you can configure the following settings:

**Voting Options**
- **Allow multiple responses**: when enabled, members can select more than one answer. When disabled, only one answer can be selected.
- **Allow contributors to add options**: when enabled, members can add their own options to the poll.

**Display Options**
- **Hide results until user votes**: when enabled, members only see the results after they have cast their own vote.
- **Show voter avatars**: when enabled, the avatars of members who voted for each option are shown in the results.

![Create a Post with a Poll](/post-with-poll.png)

### Form

A Form lets you ask members a set of questions and collect their answers in one place, for example to sign up for an event, gather dietary needs or receive requests that not everyone should read. Space admins can add a Form to a Post: you find it under **More** in **Add to Post**. Unlike other additional content, a Form can't be removed from the Post once it's created, and an existing Post can't be turned into a Form, so the responses can't get lost by accident. To remove a Form, delete the Post; the confirmation tells you how many responses will be deleted with it.

Give the Form a title and description if you like, then add your questions, up to 50. For each question you pick an answer type: **Short text** for a single line (up to 512 characters), **Long text** for a longer answer (up to 2,048 characters), **Single choice** to choose one option, or **Multiple choice** to choose one or more. A choice question needs 2 to 20 options, each with a different text. Turn on **Required** for questions that must be answered. You can still edit the questions later, also once responses have come in, and even change a question's answer type: answers that were already given stay as they were.

![Create a Post with a Form](/post-with-form.png)

In **Form settings** you choose:
- **Who can see the responses**: **Admins only**, the default, or **Space members**.
- **Responses per person**: **One response per person**, the default, or **Several responses per person**. If you switch from several to one, everyone keeps the responses they already sent, but can only send a new one after withdrawing all of them.
- **Open for responses**: turn this off to close the Form, and on again to reopen it. A closed Form accepts no new responses; the responses already sent stay.
- **Show the form collapsed by default**: only the top of the Form, with its title and description, shows until someone opens it. Everyone can still expand or collapse it themselves.

You can change all of these at any time.

![Form settings](/form-settings.png)

**Who can read the responses.** Responses are never anonymous: each one shows who sent it. With **Admins only**, the admins of the Space or Subspace can read every response; in a Subspace, the admins of the Space it belongs to can too. Everyone else sees only their own. With **Space members**, the members of the Space or Subspace where the Form is can read every response; in a Subspace, members of the parent Space who aren't members of the Subspace itself see only their own. **Space members** never means the public: visitors and people who aren't members never see other people's responses, not even in a public Space. Before sending, members see who will be able to read their answers, right next to the **Submit Form** button. You can change this setting at any time, and it always applies to all responses, including earlier ones. That's why Alkemio asks you to confirm before you switch from **Admins only** to **Space members**: members will then also read answers that were given when only admins could see them. For sensitive questions, keep **Admins only**. Responses are not contributions: they don't appear under **Contributions** or in the activity feed. If someone deletes their account, their responses stay, shown as from a **Deleted user**.

![A Form as members see it](/form-fill-in.png)

Members can fill in the Form once the Post is published, and click **Submit Form**. They can't edit a response afterwards, but they can take it back with **Withdraw**, even after the Form is closed. With one response per person, they can then send a new one. Everyone who can read all responses sees **View responses** at the top of the Form, with the number of responses. It opens an overview of all responses, where you can open each one to read it in full. Answers to a question you removed later are still shown, marked as a removed question. With **Export CSV** at the bottom of the overview you download all responses as a spreadsheet file, with one row per response: who sent it, when, and one column per question. Admins can also delete a response in the overview; members can't, even when they can read all responses.

When someone sends a response, the admins of that Space or Subspace get a notification with a link to the Form; it never contains the answers. Admins of the parent Space aren't notified, and an admin who sends a response gets only the confirmation email below. Each admin chooses how they receive it in the profile menu under **My Account**, on the **Notifications** tab, under **Space Admin Notifications**, in the row **Receive a notification when someone submits a response to a Form in a Space you administer**. In-app, email and push are all on by default. The person who sent the response always gets an email confirming it was received and saying who can read it. It doesn't repeat the answers, and there's no setting to turn it off.

You can save a Post with a Form as a template, with **Save as Template** in the Post's menu. The template keeps the title, description, questions and settings, but never the responses: a Post started from it begins with no responses. Space templates carry their Forms in the same way.

### Media Gallery

A Media Gallery lets you share a curated collection of images within a Space or Subspace.

Key Features:
- Upload multiple images at once.
- Reorder images by dragging them into the right sequence.
- Browse images in an inline gallery view, without leaving the page.
- Individual images can be downloaded.

Clicking any image opens the full gallery view.

![Create a Post with a Media Gallery](/post-with-media-gallery.png)

### Contributors and Subspaces

Space admins have two more options. **Contributors** shows the members of your community as cards, and **Subspaces** shows the Subspaces of your Space. Both keep themselves up to date as people join or Subspaces are added, or you can pick yourself which ones to show.

## Comments and Reactions

### Comments
Comments are on by default, so the community can reply to the post. This can be useful to start a discussion or ask for input.
If the post is for information only and you don't want people to reply, open **More options** while creating the Post and turn off **Allow comments**.

### Reactions
Members can react to a published post with an emoji: a heart, hugging face, clapping hands, light bulb, bullseye, check mark or rocket. Everyone has one reaction per post; pick another emoji to change it, or click your own to remove it. Click the reaction count to see who reacted.

## Contributions

Besides comments, you can ask members to add something to your post, such as links, their own posts or whiteboards. These additions are called **contributions**, and they appear in the post under **Contributions**.

You set this up in the **Responses** section when you create the Post. Choose one type: **Links & Files**, **Posts**, **Memos**, **Whiteboards**, **Documents** or a **Tasks board**. For every type you decide who can add: **Members can add** lets everyone in the community contribute, and **Admins can add** lets admins contribute. The type can't be changed after the post is created; if you need a different type, simply create a new post.

![Response options](/response-options.png)

### Links & Files

This is a way to **collect and share materials in one place**, such as articles, reports and reference documents. Members can add their own links and files, each with a description. Files added here, such as a PDF, can be opened or downloaded, but not edited together; to work on a document together, use a **Document** instead.

![Collection of links](/collection-links.png)

While creating the post, you can already add the first links under **Pre-populate collection** and decide who can add to the collection.

![Collection settings of links & files](/links-files.png)

### Posts

A collection of posts allows **structured information gathering** by providing **predefined questions or headers** that members can fill in. This is ideal for collecting insights, crowdsourcing ideas, or running structured discussions.

![Example of collection of posts](/collection-posts.png)

In the settings you can specify:
- With **Set Default Response**: a default title, guidance for members, or a template, containing for example guiding questions or a structure.
- Who can add to the collection.
- Whether members can comment on each post in the collection (**Enable comments**).

![Settings of collection of posts](/posts-settings.png)

When contributing to a collection of posts, click **Add Post**. A window opens with the guidance from the creator of the post already filled in. Fill in a title and complete the description following that guidance.

### Whiteboards

This option, shown on the post as **Call for Whiteboards**, allows members to **contribute visually**. Leads can set up a template, such as a vision board or a stakeholder map, and ask members to **add their own whiteboards**.

![Collection of Whiteboards](/call-for-whiteboards.png)

In the settings you can specify:
- With **Set Default Response**: a whiteboard that every new contribution starts from. You can draw one yourself, choose a template, or leave it empty so contributions start blank.
- Who can add to the collection.

![Settings of collection of whiteboards](/whiteboard-settings.png)

### Memos and Documents

With **Memos**, each member can add their own memo, for example a short write-up or meeting notes. With **Documents**, members add text documents, spreadsheets or presentations; this is available in Spaces where Documents are turned on.

### Tasks Board

A Tasks board is a board with columns, in which every task is a post that members move from column to column by dragging it. After creating it, use **Manage columns** in the post's menu to add, rename, reorder or remove columns.

## More Settings for the Posts

There are several settings that will help you make the most of these posts. For example, you can:

- **Edit**: After creating the post, you can still edit most settings. The kind of additional content and the contribution type can't be changed afterwards.
- **Close It for New Contributions**: In the edit window, turn off **Members can add** so only admins can still add, or turn off **Admins can add** to stop new contributions altogether. Everything that was already added stays visible.
- **Create It in Draft State**: Click **Save Draft** instead of **Post** to make it only visible for admins. When you're ready to share it with the community, choose **Publish** in the post's menu. When you post right away, you can turn on **Notify space members** to let everyone know.
- **Sort Contributions**: Adjust the order of the contributions that were added to the post.
- **Save as Template**: You can save the post as a template, which you'll then find among your Space's templates. This doesn't work yet for posts with a Document.
- **Move It Up and Down**: Reorder posts or move them to the top or bottom of the list.
- **Open It in a Larger View**: Open the post to see a clearer overview of all contributions.
- **Share It**: Share with other people inside and outside of the platform.

## Where Can You Use These Posts?

Depending on the settings of a (Sub)Space, only the **admins** or all members are able to add posts on any page! This allows them to ask the question where it makes the most sense. Let’s give you some examples:

- **A post** on the **Dashboard Tab** to ask for comments, suggestions or feedback.
- **A post with a collection of links** on the **Dashboard Tab** to share guiding materials to help members get started in this Space.
- **A post with a whiteboard containing a stakeholder map** on the **Community Tab**, so that the community can contribute with their insights about the current ecosystem.
- **A post** on the **Community Tab** to ask members to introduce themselves or suggest other people or organisations to invite.
- **A post with a collection of posts** on the **Subspaces Tab**, where members can submit ideas for creating Subspaces by filling in a few context questions.
- **A post with a collection of links** on the **Knowledge tab**, featuring relevant articles related to the topic.

Templates can give your Posts a ready-made structure and best practices. Learn more about **how to use templates** on the [Templates Page](/features/templates).
