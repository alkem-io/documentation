# Welcome to Alkemio Docs

### What is Alkemio?

The most complex challenges cannot be solved by a single organisation. They arise in the spaces between organisations, where public, private, civil society, and citizens need to work together. Alkemio exists to support change-makers doing exactly that: collaboration in the spaces between organisations.

Here's a taste of what this looks like:

- Work together using a rich toolset, guided by proven methodologies.

- Leverage the power of AI using Virtual Contributors, instant and available 24/7.

- Benefit from the network effect: find others with shared interests and new expertise.

- Backed by Alkemio Foundation, designed to benefit society.

### How does it work?

Every initiative gets its own **Space**, a dedicated digital home built around your shared goal. Set up a collaboration project, train your own Virtual Contributor, and invite your network to join you.

Personalise your Space with digital whiteboards, tailored workflows, posts, and other collaboration tools, keeping everything in one place instead of scattered across email threads and shared drives.

Spaces also connect to each other. As more initiatives join Alkemio, visibility grows across the wider ecosystem: you can see what others are working on, learn from their approach, and coordinate instead of starting from scratch.

Make progress in your business, your city, or across your network of partners.

🔗 Ready to get started? [Create your Space](/getting-started/create-space)

### Built on trust

Platforms carry real power over the communities they host, and Alkemio takes that responsibility seriously. The platform is steward-owned, so it stays purpose-driven and control is never for sale. Your data is hosted in Europe, backed by strong governance and an open-source codebase you can inspect for yourself. Read the full detail in [Platform promises](/platform-promises#introduction).
