# Delete your account

You can delete your own Alkemio account at any time. Deletion is immediate,
permanent, and cannot be undone.

## How to delete your account

1. Click your profile picture in the top right corner and go to **Settings** >
   **Security**.
2. Find **Delete account** and open it.
3. If you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to confirm it's you —
   this applies whether you sign in with a password, a passkey, or a social /
   SSO provider. Once confirmed, you're returned to the Security tab to
   continue.
4. Type your exact display name to confirm, then choose **Delete account**.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out on every device.

## If you're blocked from deleting

You can't delete your account while it still owns any of the following:

- Spaces
- Virtual Contributors
- Innovation Packs
- Innovation Hubs
- An organization you're the **sole owner** of

If any of these apply, the delete dialog lists exactly what's blocking you.
Go to **Settings** > **Account** to delete the Spaces, Virtual Contributors,
Template Packs and Custom Homepages you own. There is no self-service way to
hand an item over to someone else — email
**[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)** if you need a transfer.

An organization you solely own is not listed on that page. To clear that
blocker, appoint a second owner from that organization's own settings, or
email **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)**. Once nothing blocks
your account, deletion goes through.

## What happens to your data

**Removed immediately**: your profile, your account settings, the files stored
on your own profile and account (for example your avatar), your sign-in
identity, and all active sessions. If any part of this clean-up doesn't
complete right away, it is recorded so that our team can finish it — this is
not automatic. Contact **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)** if
you're still able to sign in after deleting your account, or if you'd like
confirmation that your uploaded files were removed.

**Not removed immediately**:

- Posts, comments, whiteboards, and other contributions you made in other
  people's Spaces stay in place but are no longer attributed to you — the
  author is simply no longer shown. In your activity feed and others',
  deleted accounts appear under the neutral label "Former member".
- Files you uploaded into other people's Spaces stay with those Spaces, along
  with the rest of your contributions there, and are no longer attributed to
  you. Email **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)** if you need them
  removed.
- Your chat identity — including your chat display name and avatar — is
  **not** removed by account deletion. Removing it is planned for a future
  update. If you'd like it removed sooner, email
  **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)**.

## Backups

Database backups are taken regularly, and copies of backups — along with a
copy of your uploaded files — are also kept in a separate off-site archive
within Europe, for disaster recovery.

Data from a deleted account can therefore still exist in those backups after
your account itself is gone. Backups are not edited to remove individual
accounts: that data goes when the backup holding it is rotated out or
removed. We can't state a fixed period for that here. If you need specifics
about backup copies of your data, email
**[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)**.

## Active subscriptions

An active subscription does not block account deletion — you can delete your
account while a subscription is in place. **Deleting your account does not
cancel your subscription.** The deletion does not contact the billing provider
at all; it only records your subscription reference so our finance team can
reconcile it afterwards. Cancel your subscription before deleting your account,
or email **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)** — once the account is
gone you can no longer reach the subscription yourself.
