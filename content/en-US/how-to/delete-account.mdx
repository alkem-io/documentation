# Delete your account

You can delete your own Alkemio account at any time. Deletion is immediate,
permanent, and cannot be undone.

## How to delete your account

1. Click your profile picture in the top right corner and go to **Settings** >
   **Security**.
2. Find **Delete account** and open it.
3. If you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to confirm it's you —
   this applies whether you sign in with a password, a passkey, or a social /
   SSO provider. Once confirmed, you're returned to the Security tab to
   continue.
4. Type your exact display name to confirm, then choose **Delete account**.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out on every device.

## If you're blocked from deleting

You can't delete your account while it still owns any of the following:

- Spaces
- Virtual Contributors
- Innovation Packs
- Innovation Hubs
- An organization you're the **sole owner** of

If any of these apply, the delete dialog lists exactly what's blocking you.
Go to **Settings** > **Account** to delete or hand over each item yourself —
for an organization, appoint a second owner or transfer ownership. If you'd
rather have us help with a transfer, email
**[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)**. Once nothing blocks your
account, deletion goes through.

## What happens to your data

**Removed immediately**: your profile, account settings, uploaded files,
sign-in identity, and all active sessions.

**Not removed immediately**:

- Posts, comments, whiteboards, and other contributions you made in other
  people's Spaces stay in place but are no longer attributed to you — they
  show as authored by "Former member".
- Your chat identity — including your chat display name and avatar — is
  **not** removed by account deletion. Removing it is planned for a future
  update. If you'd like it removed sooner, email
  **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)**.

## Backups

Database backups are taken frequently and retained for up to 30 days in our
primary backup store; a copy is also kept in a separate off-site archive
(within Europe), where it may be retained for longer. Data from a deleted
account may still exist in a backup taken before deletion until that backup
ages out.

Uploaded files (such as documents and avatars) are separately replicated to
an immutable, write-once backup store for disaster recovery. Once written,
those copies are retained for at least 30 days and **cannot be deleted on
request** during that period, even by Alkemio staff.

## Active subscriptions

An active subscription does not block account deletion — you can delete your
account while a subscription is in place. Billing is reconciled separately
after deletion.
