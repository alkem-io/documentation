# Set up two-step verification

With two-step verification, you need more than your password to sign in. After your password, you also confirm it's you with your phone or a security key. We recommend turning it on, as it makes your account safer. Even if someone gets hold of your password, they can't get into your account or your community's content.

You set it up in **My Account**, on the **Security** tab, under **Two-step verification**. You can choose an authenticator app, a passkey or security key, or both.

**Authenticator app.** To set it up, you need an authenticator app on your phone. Open the app and scan the QR code, or type in the key shown next to it, then enter the code the app shows. From then on, after your password, open the app on your phone and type in the code it shows at that moment.

**Passkey or security key.** A passkey is saved on your phone or computer, or in your password manager. A security key is a small physical key that you plug in or hold against your device. To set one up, type a name for it and click **Add a security key**. Your browser then opens a window. If you want to use a passkey, follow the steps in that window. If you want to use a physical key, choose to save it another way, pick the security key option, plug in your key and touch it.

**Recovery codes.** When you've set up your second step, also create recovery codes straight away and store them somewhere safe that you can always reach, such as a password manager. If you ever can't use your phone or key, you sign in with one of these codes. Each code works only once.

Some organisations require two-step verification for their members. If yours does, you have 14 days to set it up. Organisation admins can switch this on in the organisation's settings, under **Security**.

In the Alkemio app, you set this up in a web browser. If you can't sign in at all anymore, email **[support@alkem.io](mailto:support@alkem.io)**.
