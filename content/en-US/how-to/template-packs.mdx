# Use template packs from other organisations

The Template Library on Alkemio contains template packs contributed by various organisations, from Alkemio itself to knowledge institutes, NGOs, and methodology experts. This guide shows you how to find and use them in your Space.

## What is the Template Library?

The Template Library is a platform-wide collection of whiteboard templates, guided innovation flows, and exercises. It is available to all users and grows continuously as more organisations contribute their best practices.

The library has two parts:

1. **Innovation Packs**: the template packs. Each pack is a coherent set of templates around a theme, method or organisation, for example Gamestorming, Design. Think. Make. Break. Repeat., Circular Economy (by aRoundTable) or the Interaction Design Foundation pack.
2. **Templates**: all available templates in one place. You can search by keyword or use **Filter by type** to show, for example, only whiteboards or only Posts.

## How to explore the Template Library

Start here to get a feel for everything that's available.

1. Click the **grid icon** (top right of the platform) to open the main menu.
2. Select **Template Library**.
3. Browse the Innovation Packs, or use the search bars to find what you need.
4. Click on a pack to see the individual templates it contains, the contributing organisation, and a description.

## How to use a template from the Library in your Space

**Option 1: Add it to your Space's own templates**

1. Go to your Space settings and open the **Templates** tab. You'll see a section for each kind of template, such as Space templates, Collaboration tools and Whiteboard templates.
2. Next to the section you want, click **Add new** and choose **Select from library**.
3. In the **Template library** window, find the template you want and click **Import**. Templates listed under **Platform** come from the Template Library. Click **Done** when you're finished.
4. The template is now available to all members of your Space when creating Posts or Subspaces.

**Option 2: Use it directly while creating content**

1. When creating a Post, a Whiteboard, or a Subspace, click **Find Template**.
2. Scroll past your Space templates to find templates from the Template Library. Using this method, you'll only see templates that match the type of post you're creating.
3. Select the template to apply it immediately.

You can also start your **Community Guidelines** from a template; see [Create a Space](/getting-started/create-space#community).

## Want to contribute your own templates?

Anyone can share templates in the library to support innovation. If you're interested, reach out via the forum or email **support@alkem.io**.
